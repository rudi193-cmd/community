# Skill: Understanding Fractions

**ID:** math-g3-fractions-001
**Domain:** Mathematics
**Grade:** 3
**Prerequisites:** math-g2-equal-parts-001

## Description

Understanding that a fraction represents equal parts of a whole.

## CCSS Alignment

3.NF.A.1 - Understand a fraction as a quantity formed by parts of a whole
3.NF.A.2 - Understand a fraction on a number line

## Proficiency indicators

| Level | What the student can do |
|---|---|
| Emerging | Identifies halves and quarters with manipulatives |
| Developing | Names fractions from diagrams |
| Proficient | Places fractions on a number line |
| Advanced | Compares and orders fractions with different denominators |
