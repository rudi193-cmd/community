# Fractions on a Number Line

**Grade:** 3
**Domain:** Mathematics
**CCSS:** 3.NF.A.2
**Language:** English
**Time:** 45 minutes

## Learning objective

Students will place fractions on a number line between 0 and 1.

## AI-assisted activity

Use an AI tool to generate 10 number line problems at varying difficulty.
Ask students to explain their reasoning aloud before writing their answer.

## Materials

- Printed number lines (template in /resources/templates)
- Whiteboard markers

## Assessment

Students place 5 fractions correctly on a number line with 80% accuracy.
