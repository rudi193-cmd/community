# Community Code of Conduct

This community is for K-12 educators, researchers, and builders.
We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive environment for everyone.

## Our standards

- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive feedback graciously
- Focus on what is best for students and educators

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to admin@emergingrule.com.
The Emerging Rule team reserves the right to remove any contribution
that does not meet these standards.
