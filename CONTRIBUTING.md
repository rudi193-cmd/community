# Contributing to Emerging Rule Community

Thank you for contributing. This repository grows through educators,
researchers, and builders sharing what works in K-12 education.

## Ground rules

- Be respectful and constructive
- All content must be appropriate for K-12 educational contexts
- Bilingual contributions in English and Spanish are especially welcome
- Cite sources for any research references

## Types of contributions

Lesson ideas
Add a markdown file to the right lessons/ subfolder.
Name it: grade-topic-yourname.md
Example: grade3-fractions-martinez.md

Skill taxonomy
Add or refine a skill definition in skill-taxonomy/.
Approved contributions may be integrated into the LevelShip AI engine.

Templates
Add reusable classroom templates to resources/templates/.

Research
Add annotated references to resources/research/.

Showcase
Share a project or implementation in showcase/.

## Pull request format

Title: [TYPE] Brief description
Example: [LESSON] Grade 5 data literacy activity in Spanish

Body: One paragraph describing what you added and why it is useful.

## Review

All contributions are reviewed within 48 hours.
Approved contributions are merged and credited to the contributor.
