Creature Lab

Eight hands-on computer science lessons for grades 4–8. Build a virtual pet from scratch, wire up interactions, code a network, and learn the systems that make programs work.

Grades 4–6

Design Your Creature

Before any code, draw and name your pet. What colors? What makes it happy? What's its superpower?

Grades 4–6

Your First Pet

Tell the computer how to draw your creature. Canvas, shapes, animation loops — the full pipeline.

Grades 5–7

Buttons & Interaction

Click a button, something runs, your pet reacts. Build the action system that brings your creature to life.

Grades 5–7

Stats and Decay

Your pet has needs. Food, energy, happiness — they drain over time. Now your pet needs you.

Grades 5–7

Save Your Pet

Close the browser and come back later — your pet remembers. Learn how localStorage makes data persist.

Grades 6–8

The Living Network

Two pets running in two tabs. How do they send letters? localStorage as a shared mailbox — real distributed systems.

Grades 6–8

The Hub

A dashboard that brings four pets together. CSS Grid, iframes, scaling — real interface design patterns.

Grades 6–8

Flash the Watch

Take your pet to hardware. Upload C++ to a microcontroller watch. Your code, compiled and running on a device.
