## What this is

A **six-lesson example arc** for grades 9–12, plus a **K–12 companion parable** (*The Scribe Who Forgot His Dreams*). Posted as an **example submission** so educators and maintainers can review structure, tone, and fit before any “official” merge decision.

Each lesson meets the **posole criterion**: usable by a teacher with 30 students, no devices required for the core activity.

## Arc (one line per unit)

1. **Mechanics** — prediction ≠ knowledge  
2. **Representation** — whose voices trained the model  
3. **Data** — consent and who benefits  
4. **Philosophy** — simulation vs understanding (pairs with Scribe)  
5. **Practice** — evaluate confident wrong answers  
6. **Agency** — student manifestos
